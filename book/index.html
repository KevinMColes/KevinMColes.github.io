Inside the story

The life behind
the reliable one.

Long before Kevin M. Coles built a career around consequential decisions, he was a kid learning to figure things out for himself.

The One Who Would Be Okay follows the experiences that shaped him: an often solitary childhood, the loss of his father, the climb into enterprise technology, financial collapse, and the decision to rebuild.

Fatherhood gives the story its most personal stakes. What do you carry forward? What ends with you? And what will the people you love remember about having you there?
