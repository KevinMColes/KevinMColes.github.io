Technology & leadership

Decided by Accident

For owners, executives, boards, and business associations.

A renewal nobody questioned. Software one department bought. A temporary workaround that became permanent. Together, those choices become the technology a business depends on.

Drawing on enterprise architecture and his work through Coles Technical Group, Kevin M. Coles examines how leaders take ownership of those decisions: what to fund, what to question, and who stays accountable.

Leave with: sharper questions for providers, clearer ownership of major decisions, and a way to connect technology spending to business priorities.

Personal story

The One Who Would Be Okay

For conferences, organizations, and community audiences.

Some people become dependable long before anyone asks whether they should have to be.

Kevin M. Coles draws from his memoir: growing up largely on his own, losing his father, building a career, financial collapse, and becoming a father determined to be present. The talk follows what those experiences changed about responsibility and success.

Leave with: a different way to think about the dependable people in your life, and what showing up for them actually means.

Safety & awareness

Strong. Prepared. Confident.

For workplaces, women’s groups, and community organizations.

Raising Tactical Daughters began when Kevin M. Coles’s first daughter was born. Today, that responsibility extends to both of his daughters.

Kevin M. Coles brings that work into a practical conversation about situational awareness, de-escalation, boundaries, and trusting the signals people too often dismiss. The focus is on recognizing trouble early and having options.

Leave with: everyday ways to notice changes in your surroundings, set boundaries, and think through your next move.
