Technology & leadership

Decided by Accident

For owners, executives, boards, and business associations.

Most businesses did not design the technology environment they now depend on. They inherited it through renewals, urgent purchases, workarounds, and decisions nobody remembers making.

Kevin M. Coles shows leaders how to recognize those accumulated choices, challenge recommendations, and bring business priorities back into technology direction.

Audience takeaway: better questions for the next technology investment—and a clearer understanding of who owns the answer.

Personal story

The One Who Would Be Okay

For conferences, organizations, and community audiences.

What happens when people assume you will manage because you always have?

Drawing from his memoir, Kevin M. Coles speaks about growing up largely on his own, losing his father, building a career, financial collapse, and rebuilding a life around what matters.

Audience takeaway: a more honest conversation about strength, responsibility, and the people behind the roles.

Safety & awareness

Strong. Prepared. Confident.

For workplaces, women’s groups, and community organizations.

Confidence grows when people have practical options.

Drawing on Raising Tactical Daughters, Kevin M. Coles discusses situational awareness, de-escalation, boundaries, and trusting the signals people too often dismiss.

Audience takeaway: practical ways to notice risk earlier and make more deliberate choices.
