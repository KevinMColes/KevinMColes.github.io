Short biography

Kevin M. Coles is an Arizona-based speaker, author, and technology leader. He is the founder of Coles Technical Group and Raising Tactical Daughters, and the author of The One Who Would Be Okay. His work spans enterprise technology leadership, practical safety education, and writing about responsibility, fatherhood, and rebuilding.
